Payment Instructions

Use one of the methods below to send your payment after receiving your confirmed payment amount.

Payment recipient – Airtel Money Kenya

+254 751 896 015

Recipient: Global Online Visa Applications. Please verify the recipient's registered name before sending money.

Method 1: M-Pesa to Airtel Money

  1. Open M-Pesa on your Safaricom line or dial *334#.
  2. Choose the option for sending money to another mobile network, if available.
  3. Select Airtel Money where that option is offered.
  4. Enter the recipient number: 0751896015.
  5. Enter the confirmed payment amount.
  6. Check the recipient details carefully before confirming with your M-Pesa PIN.
  7. Keep the M-Pesa confirmation message and transaction code.

M-Pesa menu options may change. If the other-network option is unavailable, contact Safaricom or use another supported payment method. Do not send money to an unverified number.

Method 2: Bank transfer to Airtel Money

  1. Open your bank's mobile banking app, internet banking or visit a branch.
  2. Look for Mobile Money, Airtel Money or Bank-to-Wallet transfer.
  3. Select Airtel Money as the receiving network, if supported by your bank.
  4. Enter the recipient number: +254 751 896 015.
  5. Enter the confirmed payment amount.
  6. Verify the recipient details and any transaction charges before confirming.
  7. Keep your bank transaction reference or receipt.

Bank transfer options differ between banks. Confirm with your bank that it supports transfers to Airtel Money before making payment. Do not use bank account details that have not been officially provided to you.

Method 3: Sending money from outside Kenya

Applicants outside Kenya can check international remittance providers that support Airtel Money wallet payouts to Kenya.

  • Remitly
  • WorldRemit
  • Western Union
  1. Choose Kenya as the receiving country.
  2. Check whether Airtel Money or Airtel mobile wallet payout is available.
  3. Enter the recipient number: +254 751 896 015.
  4. Enter the recipient name exactly as requested by the provider.
  5. Review the exchange rate, fees and amount to be received.
  6. Confirm the transfer only after checking the recipient details.
  7. Keep the transfer confirmation and reference number.

International payout availability depends on your country and the remittance provider. If Airtel Money is not listed as a payout option, do not send the money using an alternative method without first contacting us.

Important: Always wait for your confirmed payment amount before paying. Sending money does not automatically mark an application as Paid. Our administrator must verify the payment.
