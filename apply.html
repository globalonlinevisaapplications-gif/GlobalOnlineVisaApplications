IMPORTANT NOTICE: Visa processing times displayed on Global Online Visa Applications are provided for general information and guidance only. Processing times may vary depending on the destination country, visa category, applicant's nationality, application completeness, embassy or consulate workload, security checks, public holidays and changes in immigration policies. The processing times are not guaranteed approval or decision dates. Actual processing may be shorter or longer than the published estimates. Immigration authorities and embassies have sole responsibility for visa decisions and official processing timelines. Applicants are advised to verify the latest requirements and processing times through the relevant government immigration website or embassy before making travel arrangements. Global Online Visa Applications is a private visa consultancy and is not a government agency, embassy or consulate.
