Visa Application Processing Time

We aim to make your application process clear and convenient. Here are the main stages after you begin your application.

1

Document Review

Our target is to review submitted documents within 48 hours after receiving all required documents.

2

Application Preparation

We check the application information and prepare the required documents for the next stage.

3

Application Submission

Once the application is complete and verified, it can proceed to the relevant submission process.

4

Government Decision

The embassy or immigration authority makes the final decision. Waiting times depend on the destination and visa category.

Important information: Our 48-hour target applies to our document review, not government visa approval. Actual visa processing and decision times vary by country, visa type, application completeness and the relevant immigration authority. We cannot guarantee approval or a government decision within a specific number of days.
